Tips · Corrections · Partnerships

Contact Deer Park Report.

Use the form below for tips, corrections, classroom and community partnerships, and outreach. For active emergencies, call 911. For environmental complaints, the TCEQ complaint line is faster than us.

Submissions go through Netlify Forms. We do not share your email. We aim to respond to corrections and tips within a few business days.

Faster routes

  • Active emergency or active release: call 911.
  • Air-quality or environmental complaint: TCEQ complaint form or EPA report.
  • Worker safety concern: OSHA complaint form.
  • Shelter-in-place / community alerts: City of Deer Park, Deer Park ISD.
