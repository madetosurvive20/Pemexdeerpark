About Deer Park Report

An independent community hub for Deer Park, Texas.

Deer Park Report is a small, independent publication covering the Pemex Deer Park refinery, the Houston Ship Channel, and the Deer Park petrochemical corridor — for residents, families, plant workers, and anyone trying to understand what happens behind the fenceline.

What we cover

  • Air Quality — live AQI, PM2.5, ozone, and PM10 from the EPA AirNow / TCEQ monitoring network for the Houston-Galveston-Brazoria region, plus health guidance for residents.
  • News — verified coverage from Reuters, the Houston Chronicle, ABC13, Click2Houston, S&P Global, and other outlets.
  • Jobs — curated process operator, engineering, technician, EHS, and trades openings across PMX Deer Park, LyondellBasell, ExxonMobil Baytown, INEOS, and the Ship Channel turnaround pool.
  • Incidents — a public-record timeline of incidents at and near the refinery and surrounding corridor, sourced from TCEQ, EPA, OSHA, and U.S. Coast Guard filings.
  • Ship Channel — vessel activity, terminals, and the role of the Port of Houston in the regional energy economy.
  • For Kids & Families — a free, classroom-friendly explainer of air quality, refinery basics, and shelter-in-place safety.

How we source data

Air-quality numbers come live from the EPA AirNow public feed, which aggregates TCEQ continuous monitoring stations across Harris County and the Ship Channel. Incident records are drawn from public TCEQ Air Emission Event Reports, EPA filings, OSHA enforcement records, and U.S. Coast Guard pollution reports. News coverage is aggregated from verified outlets via Google News.

Editorial principles

  • Independence. Deer Park Report is not affiliated with Pemex, Petróleos Mexicanos, or any of its subsidiaries, contractors, or partners. We have no editorial relationship with the operator.
  • Public-record sourcing. Every incident on this site can be cross-checked through the regulatory agency cited.
  • Plain language. We try to explain technical refinery and air-quality concepts in language a Deer Park resident can follow without a chemistry degree — including a dedicated kids' page.
  • Corrections. If you spot a factual error, please tell us. We will correct and date-stamp the change.

Get in touch

For corrections, tips, or community partnerships, visit our contact page. For active emergencies, call 911 or contact the TCEQ complaint line.
