Pemex Completes $380M Overhaul at Deer Park — Largest Turnaround in a Decade

The Deer Park facility emerges from its most extensive scheduled maintenance cycle in ten years, signalling a deepened long-term commitment by Pemex on the Gulf Coast.

Staff Report  ·  March 18, 2025  ·  Deer Park, Harris County, Texas

The Pemex Deer Park refinery has successfully completed a comprehensive $380 million reliability turnaround, upgrading key atmospheric distillation units, catalytic cracking infrastructure, and environmental control systems across the 340,000 barrel-per-day facility on the Houston Ship Channel.

The investment is expected to extend operational reliability well into the next decade while improving per-barrel margins and reducing emissions intensity. Industry analysts describe it as the most significant capital deployment at the site since Pemex's full acquisition of the facility.

The turnaround, which temporarily reduced throughput during Q3 2024, has concluded with refinery utilization returning to above 91 percent of nameplate capacity, according to pipeline flow data reviewed by this publication.

The scale of the investment underscores Pemex's strategic commitment to the Deer Park asset as a cornerstone of its North American downstream portfolio — a signal that carries particular weight given ongoing restructuring pressures in its Mexican upstream operations.
