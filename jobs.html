Training & apprenticeships

Most refinery and plant employers in the Deer Park corridor recruit from a narrow set of feeder programs. The two strongest:

  • San Jacinto College — Process Technology: AAS degree and certificate. The primary pipeline into refinery operator roles for PMX, LyondellBasell, ExxonMobil, and INEOS.
  • Lee College (Baytown) — Industrial Trades: Welding, instrumentation, electrical, industrial maintenance. Strong placement record with ExxonMobil Baytown and Ship Channel employers.

Heads up — Deer Park and Pasadena are different markets

Deer Park (PMX) and Pasadena (LyondellBasell) sit minutes apart but recruit and pay differently. Don't filter by city alone — search both, plus La Porte for terminal/pipeline and Baytown for ExxonMobil.
